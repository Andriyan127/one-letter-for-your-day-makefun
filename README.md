## The hidden word: QUEASY

A day my stomach vetoed everything — the street food at noon that
seemed like a great idea, the third coffee, the bumpy bus ride home.
Not sick, not fine either: just queasy, all day, in that very specific
green-around-the-gills way everyone has lived through.

| W / whirlwind | ✅ | 8 — spinning, busy | 7 — pleasant, not funny | 8 — real | 9 — W sounds like whirling | 32 |

**Q / queasy wins:** the rarest letter of the shortlist (instantly
surprising on the page), a sharp physical picture that lands at once,
funny without being performed, and honest — a queasy day is a day
everyone recognises. Z, L, and H were already taken by other entries,
so Q also stands alone in the field.
